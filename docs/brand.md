## Tagline

# «Nuevo para todos. Inolvidable para cada uno.» [New for everyone. Unforgettable for each one.]

**Alternatives for campaigns and onboarding** (more provocative):

- «¿Otra vez a la playa de siempre? Ni de broma.» [The same beach again? No way.]
- «Tus amigos ya fueron a París. Vosotros vais a flipar.» [Your friends already went to Paris. You're going to freak out.]
- «Ve donde nadie ha ido. Vuelve enamorado.» [Go where nobody has been. Come back in love.]
- «Basta de "ya fui". Toca "no te lo vas a creer".» [Enough with "been there". Time for "you won't believe this".]

## Promise

Discover **together** a destination or route that **nobody in the group knows** and that fits the tastes of **everyone**.

## How it delivers

Each person marks where they have been and where they would like to go → matchMap compares the maps and calculates the **group match** → it proposes destinations that none of them has visited, and explains why.

## Audience

- **Initial (MVP):** friends and couples who want to decide together on their next getaway: a place none of them has been to, that everyone likes.
- **Advanced (phase 2+):** the adventurous traveller (25–60 years old) who avoids crowded places, looks for authenticity and calm, and enjoys meeting people along the way. Also digital nomads (a "I want to move" mode).

## Core emotion

**"Deciding together without arguing"**: the moment of "this one is good for all of us!".

## Ego (engagement driver)

The number of countries and cities visited is **pride**: always in view, in large type, and used to **challenge** friends (ranking). Good-natured, never humiliating.

## Personality

| It is… | …but not |
|---|---|
| **Companionable**: the friend who always proposes the plan | intrusive (it is not a likes-based social network) |
| **Smart**: recommends well | technical (no tables, formulas or jargon) |
| **Adventurous**: new places | reckless (it gives trust and reliable data) |

## Tone of voice

Informal, using "tú", **with humour and a hook**. The fact first, then the joke. Serious and precise when it matters: errors, privacy and visas.

- «Tú, Marta y Javi: 91 % de match con Eslovenia. Nadie ha ido. Ya no hay excusa.» [You, Marta and Javi: 91 % match with Slovenia. Nobody has been. No more excuses.]
- «Vas 2.º del grupo. Marta te saca 8 ciudades.» [You're 2nd in the group. Marta is 8 cities ahead of you.]
- «Tu mapa está más vacío que un aeropuerto a las 4 a. m.» [Your map is emptier than an airport at 4 a.m.]

## Design compass

- **Fresh:** airy, bright colours that do not shout, smooth animations and microcopy with humour.
- **Useful:** the map and the match % are understood in 2 seconds. Few screens, zero tables.
- **Visible ego:** the counter is always at the top.

## Visual identity (summary; details in design.md)

- **Direction:** *Atlas nocturno* (Night Atlas). Muted world-map colours + **one single turquoise accent** (`#0F7C80` day / `#2DD4BF` night). Modern, bold, designed for the night too.
- **Typography:** Sora (display) + Inter (text).
- **Logo:** a map folded like an accordion whose top edge draws two M's (**M**atch + **M**ap). The central fold in the accent colour = what you share.
- **Requirements:** responsive (mobile first), WCAG AA, day and night modes.

## Competition

A deliberate decision: not analysed yet, so that it does not bias the vision. **Review it after the first designs** as a check of differentiation (known references: Visited, Been, Nomad List).
